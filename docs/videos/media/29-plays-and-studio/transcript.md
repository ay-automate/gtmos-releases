### What a play is

A play is a declarative automation: a trigger, a filter, and steps. Each step is an operation, one of the typed, authorized actions your console and agent already use. That's the core guarantee: a play can never do what a person in that role couldn't, and your guardrails apply to every step.

A trigger is an event, a schedule, or a segment. Open a play in Engines, Plays to see its trigger, filter and steps. Schedule plays fan out over an audience, like every deal stuck in a stage. Twenty five run at once by default, one per member per spell.

Here's the rule that protects your data. Event plays never fire on bulk writes. An import or a sync can't set off a flood of follow ups at once.

### The objection

Here's the doubt: an automation touching my CRM while I'm asleep? Every step is an operation, so a role's limits apply. Event plays ignore bulk writes. Sends stay proposals that a different person approves.
