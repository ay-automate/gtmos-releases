# Voice, proof and claims

Clip 19, mixed, about 5 minutes. Ladder level: Build.

## Transcript

Through-line: Say only what you can prove, checked when proposed and again when sent.

### The hook and the promise

Your numbers now reconcile to one revenue truth. Now do the same for your words. A message that promises a result nobody can prove is a liability. Here, every claim is checked against proof you approved.

In this clip you'll set your voice and the claims you never make. You'll build a proof library, choose what happens to an unbacked claim, and mine your customers' own words.

### The stakes

Without a gate, Orbit drafts a first touch that says Northwind doubled their pipeline. Nobody approved that number, and Northwind never agreed to be named. Say only what you can prove, checked when proposed and again when sent.

### First: voice

First, voice. In the console: Settings, Brand and compliance. Name the voice and give it tone words. List forbidden claims, the promises your brand never makes, each with a reason that shows on a blocked draft. Add words to avoid and a few voice samples. Drafts imitate their style and never copy them. When you save, the rules are signed, and you can paste any draft into the checker to see its violations.

### Second: the proof library

Second, the proof library. In the console: Settings, Proof library. It holds what you can back up: case studies, metrics, testimonials and logos. Upload a file or paste the text. Your model proposes the claims, and each one must quote the source word for word, with its numbers inside the quote, or it's dropped. You review the claims before anyone approves.

Set a permission on each source: public, anonymized or internal only. A customer whose proof isn't public can never be named, whatever the draft says. Set an expiry date, then propose activation, and a different person approves. Only active proof backs a claim: approved by a second person, not expired, and not internal only.

Operator tip: make expiry a habit. Each source shows its state: active, waiting for a second person, or expired. At a glance you see what backs your copy right now, and what to renew first.

### Third: the gate

Third, the gate, the part that makes this safe. Every factual claim in outbound content is checked against active proof: a statistic, a result, a named customer or an outcome. It's checked twice, when the message is proposed, and again right before it's sent, under the policy in force then. The gate covers outreach messages and sequence steps, SEO page publishes, and meeting invites and cancellation notes. If proof is archived or expires between approval and send, the send fails rather than going out unbacked.

Each claim gets one of three verdicts. Backed by proof, with its source shown to the approver. Close to proof, meaning the numbers and direction match an approved claim and enough of the wording does. And No proof. Gotcha: close never counts as backed. You set the close match threshold in the claims check settings.

Then choose what happens to an unbacked claim. In the console: Settings, Proof library, Claims check. Block is the default: the proposal stops until the copy is fixed or the proof is added. Route to the approver lets it through, but the approver must acknowledge each claim in the Claims panel before approving. The proposer, or an agent, can't acknowledge for them. Setting this policy is human only.

Or ask your agent: "Add this case study to the proof library and propose it for activation". It returns an approval number, and if you ask it to draft and propose a message, it reports the claims verdicts. It can't approve an activation, set the claims policy, or approve a phrase.

### Fourth: customer language

Fourth, customer language. In the console: Settings, Proof library, Customer language, then Find phrases. It reads your call transcripts, classified replies and won deal notes, skips private calls, and lists the words customers used. Each phrase shows its source, and a person approves or rejects every one. Approved phrases ground drafts in how your customers talk, and rejected ones are never used. Or ask your agent: "Find customer phrases from our recent calls and replies for review".

One honest limit. Drafts today use your brand voice, your proof and your customers' phrases, but not yet a full company context definition. For anything beyond those three, a person reviews the draft.

### The objection

Here's the doubt: a gate that blocks claims will stall outreach. It stops only factual claims with no proof, and you choose what happens next. Add the proof once, and every later draft can use it. Or route to the approver, who acknowledges each claim by name. Either way, nothing unbacked goes out silently.

### Recap and close

Quick pass: Set your voice and forbidden claims. Build proof with a permission and an expiry. Let the gate check every claim, twice. Say only what you can prove, checked when proposed and again when sent.

Your voice, your proof, and a gate that reads both, with a person on every exception. Add one real case study to the library today. The next clip is consent, suppression and privacy.

## Where to click

- Settings > Brand and compliance > Brand safety: voice name, tone words, forbidden claims with a reason, words to avoid, voice samples, the draft checker
- Settings > Proof library > Proof: upload or paste, review claims, permission, expiry, propose activation
- Settings > Proof library > Claims check: Block unbacked claims or Route to the approver, close match threshold
- Settings > Proof library > Customer language: Find phrases, approve or reject each phrase
- Approvals > the approval card > Claims panel (the approver acknowledges each claim in route mode)
