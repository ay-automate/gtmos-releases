# Clip 05: Blueprints and motions

## Transcript

### The hook and the promise

Setup drafted everything from evidence last clip, and it suggested a blueprint for you. Pick a motion now, and a working system is drafted in one change: personas, scoring, suppression, lanes and plays.

Over the next few minutes you'll know what a blueprint installs, how to preview and apply one, and what stands between approved and live.

### The stakes

Leo wires outbound one setting at a time. Weeks later the scoring, the suppression list and the sequences disagree, and the change history is a guess.

A motion is the way you go to market. A blueprint is a shipped template that seeds a whole motion in one ChangeSet, a bundle of changes reviewed together. Blueprints are general templates, never forked per customer. Hold on to this: a blueprint is a whole motion in one change, and approved is not live.

### Fourth: approve, sign, promote

Fourth, approve, sign, promote. Sophia opens the approval card: the frozen payload as a diff, the impact preview, and who proposed it. She can't approve her own proposals, and neither can the person an agent acts for. When she approves, the change executes in dev.

Fifth, customize. Values your organization owns come from your approved setup cards. ICP weights, stages, customer types, suppression lists and the daily cap are adapted to what you approved, not left as template defaults.

### The objection

The objection: someone else's template running my go to market? Not until you say so. It lands as drafts in dev, adapted to your approved values, and engines ignore drafts. They read only the signed version.
